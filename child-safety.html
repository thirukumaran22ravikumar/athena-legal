Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Our standard

Athena AI - Learning App (developer: CarePulse) has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Any such content or behaviour, including grooming, sexualising minors, or asking minors for images or contact, is strictly prohibited.

How we prevent and act

  • The app is for users aged 18 and over.
  • Users cannot send images or files to each other. Practice calls are voice-only, and users can end, block and report any call partner in the app.
  • Users can report any AI reply from inside the app.
  • Reports are reviewed by our team. Accounts involved in CSAE are removed immediately and permanently.
  • We report confirmed CSAM and child exploitation to the relevant authorities, including India's National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in), and comply with applicable child-safety laws.

Report a concern

In the app: after a call, tap Report; for AI replies, tap Report under the reply.

By email (child-safety contact): carepulsesupport@gmail.com. We act on child-safety reports as a priority.
