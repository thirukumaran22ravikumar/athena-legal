Who we are

Athena AI - Learning App ("Athena AI", "we", "us") is a language-learning app published on Google Play by the developer CarePulse, based in India. This policy explains what personal data the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It applies to the Android app and these web pages.

Contact / grievance officer: carepulsesupport@gmail.com

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who process it for us:

  • Supabase: database, authentication and server functions (stores your account and learning data).
  • AI model providers: receive the text of your AI conversations and tool requests to generate replies. We send only what is needed for the reply.
  • Speech-to-text provider: receives your voice recording to transcribe it, then returns text.
  • Agora: carries the live audio of practice calls between learners.
  • Google: Sign in with Google (if you choose it, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us; we never see your Google password), Google Play Billing (subscriptions) and Google AdMob (ads on the free plan). Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/privacy.

We may also disclose data if required by Indian law or to protect the safety of users.

Ads and your choices

Free-plan users see ads from Google AdMob. Where required, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change it any time in Profile → Ad privacy choices. Paid plans do not show ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile and linked data (progress, AI chats, coins, reports you made) are deleted from our live systems immediately, and from backups within 30 days. Records we must keep by law (for example purchase records held by Google) are kept for the period the law requires.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws, you can:

  • see and correct your data (Profile → Edit profile);
  • remove Athena AI's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections (if you signed in with Google);
  • delete your account and data (how to delete);
  • withdraw consent for personalised ads (Profile → Ad privacy choices);
  • ask us any question or raise a grievance at carepulsesupport@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

Age

Athena AI is meant for users aged 18 and over, because it includes live practice calls between learners. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to the database is restricted by row-level security so each user can read only their own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
