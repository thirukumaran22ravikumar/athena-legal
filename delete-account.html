Delete your account

Athena AI - Learning App · by CarePulse

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Athena AI and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile (bottom right).
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted right away.

App-இல்: Profile → Account → Delete account → உறுதிப்படுத்துங்கள். உடனே நீக்கப்படும்.

Option 2: by email

If you cannot open the app, email carepulsesupport@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" from the email address you signed up with (for Google sign-in, your Gmail address). We delete the account within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, photo, languages, level, settings
  • Learning progress, answers, mistakes, review schedule, streaks, XP and badges
  • AI tutor conversations and tool history
  • Coins and coin history, call history, online status, blocks, and reports you made

Data is removed from our live systems immediately and from backups within 30 days.

What may be kept

  • Records of Google Play purchases, which Google keeps under its own policy and tax law.
  • Our admin audit log may keep a note that an account was deleted (with the email address) so we can answer later questions.

Signed in with Google?

Deleting your Athena AI account removes the data we hold. To also remove Athena AI's access to your Google account, open myaccount.google.com/connections, choose Athena AI - Learning App and select Delete all connections. Your Google account itself is not affected.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a paid plan. Cancel it first in Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Athena AI → Cancel.
